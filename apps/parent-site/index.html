A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your
next step.

What can I actually use today?

The personal planner works now: create quests, mark them complete, earn XP, explore domain templates, and calculate a sample budget. Data is saved on this browser only. This site is a prototype alongside the existing Level-Up Life repository.

Is this for me or my family?

This website is for adults and parents. Use it to plan your own growth and explore the family concept. Shared family accounts, child account linking, and parent approvals belong to the separate application and are not connected here.

Does XP have a cash value?

No. XP measures progress inside the planner. This version does not send money, pay allowance, hold crypto, or connect a wallet. The budget tool is a planning exercise.

Will my plan follow me to another device?

Not yet. Plans are stored in this browser. You can export a JSON backup from the planner. Account sign-in and cloud sync require connecting and verifying the repository’s Supabase application.

What does it cost?

This personal prototype is free to try. There is no paid plan or subscription on this site. Pricing for future shared family features has not been set.
